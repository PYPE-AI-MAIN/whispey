A voice call uses three services in a loop. Speech-to-text (STT) turns what the caller says into text. The language model (LLM) decides what to reply. Text-to-speech (TTS) turns the reply into a voice.

You choose all three in the row at the top left of **Agent Config**.

## Primary and Fallback

A small switch at the top of the row has two buttons, **Primary** and **Fallback**.

- **Primary** shows your main LLM, STT and TTS.
- **Fallback** shows a second set. A switch labelled **Enable Fallback** turns the second set on or off. Your choices are kept when you switch it off.

The fallback pickers work the same way as the primary ones. This page does not describe when a fallback is used, because the screen does not say.

### Azure OpenAI settings

Choosing **Azure OpenAI** opens **Configure Azure OpenAI**.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Model** | Pick one of the listed models. | GPT 4.1 Mini |
| **Azure Endpoint** | The address of your Azure resource. | `https://pype-azure-openai.cognitiveservices.azure.com/` |
| **API Version** | The Azure API version. | `2024-12-01-preview` |

Click **Save Configuration** to keep them.

### Temperature

Next to the model button is a settings button with a slider from 0 to 1. The ends are labelled **Focused** and **Creative**, and a word shows the level: **Precise**, **Balanced** or **Creative**.

Lower values give steadier answers. Higher values give more varied answers. New agents start at 0.3.

:::note
For GPT 5, GPT 5 Mini, GPT 5 Nano, GPT 5.6 Terra and GPT 5.6 Luna the slider is locked at 1. The tooltip says "Selected model only supports temperature of 1".
:::

## Choose the speech-to-text service

Click the STT button to open **Configure STT Provider**. Choose a provider under **Choose STT Provider**, set it up, then click **Apply Settings**.

| Provider | Models |
|---|---|
| **OpenAI** | Whisper v1 |
| **Deepgram** | Flux (English), Flux (Multilingual), Nova 3, Nova 3 Medical (English only), Nova 2 |
| **Sarvam AI** | Saarika v2.5, Saaras v3, Saaras v4 |
| **Smallest AI** | Pulse |

Each provider has a **Language** list. The list depends on the model. Deepgram Nova 3 and Nova 2 include a multilingual option. Sarvam has Indian languages and, for Saaras, **Auto-detect (Saaras only)**. Smallest AI includes two auto-detect choices, one for North Indic languages and one for South Indic languages.

### Deepgram settings

**Flux models** show a **Turn Detection** group.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **EOT Threshold** | How sure Deepgram must be that the caller has finished. Range 0.5 to 0.9. |
| **Eager EOT** | Starts the reply earlier to cut delay. Range 0.3 to 0.9. Empty means off. |
| **Silence Timeout** | Milliseconds of silence before the turn ends. Range 500 to 30000. |

**Nova models** show these settings.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Silence Timeout** | Milliseconds of silence that mark the end of speech. |
| **Punctuate** | Adds punctuation and capital letters. On by default. |
| **Filler Words** | Keeps words such as "um" and "uh". On by default. |
| **Smart Format** | Formats numbers, dates, money and web addresses. Off by default. |
| **Numerals** | Turns spoken numbers into digits. Off by default. |
| **Profanity Filter** | Replaces rude words with asterisks. Off by default. |

**Key Terms** appears for Nova 3 and the Flux models. Type a word or a name, such as a product name, and press Enter. Deepgram then listens harder for it.

### Sarvam Saaras settings

For Saaras v3 and v4, a **Mode** list appears.

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Transcribe** | Standard transcription in the original language. |
| **Translate** | Translates speech from any Indian language to English. |
| **Verbatim** | Word for word, keeping filler words and spoken numbers. |
| **Transliterate** | Writes speech in Roman letters. |
| **Codemix** | English words stay in English. Indian words use their own script. |

These models also show **High VAD Sensitivity** (faster end-of-speech detection for quicker turn-taking) and **First Turn Min Speech Frames** (1 to 15). A lower number helps catch a quick, soft first reply such as "yes".

## Choose the voice

Click the voice button to open the TTS window. A rail on the left lists the providers. Pick one, choose a voice, adjust the settings, then click **Apply Settings**.

| Provider | What it offers |
|---|---|
| **Sarvam AI** (Bulbul, Indic voices) | A list of voices with a search box and a model list: **bulbul:v4 flash (beta)**, **bulbul:v3 (beta)** and **bulbul:v2** |
| **ElevenLabs** (your voice library) | A search box that also accepts a voice ID, and a model list |
| **Google TTS** (cloud voices) | A search box and a **Voice Name** such as `en-IN-Wavenet-A` |
| **Raya** (Bakbak, Indian languages and English) | Its own voice list and settings |

Hover over a voice and click the play button to hear a preview.

### Sarvam voice settings

| Setting | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Target Language Code** | List | The language the voice speaks. |
| **Pace** | 0.5 to 2.0 | Slow (0.5x) to fast (2.0x). |
| **Loudness** | 0.5 to 2.0 | Only used with bulbul:v2. |
| **Pitch** | -20 to 20 | Only used with bulbul:v2. |
| **Enable Preprocessing** | On or off | Tidies numbers, dates and English words in mixed-language text. Only used with bulbul:v2. |

When a setting is not used by the chosen model, the screen shows a "not applied" badge and greys it out.

### ElevenLabs voice settings

| Setting | Range | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Voice ID** | Type or pick | Set per agent |
| **Language** | List | `en` |
| **Model** | Multilingual v2, Turbo v2.5, Flash v2.5, Eleven v3 | Flash v2.5 |
| **Similarity Boost** | 0 to 1 | 1 |
| **Stability** | 0 to 1 | 0.8 |
| **Style** | 0 to 1 | 1 |
| **Speed** | 0.25 to 4.0 | 1.05 |
| **Use Speaker Boost** | On or off | On |

The last five are under **Advanced Configuration**.

### Raya voice settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Model** | **M1** (an alternative model with its own voice set) or **Standard** (the default synthesis model). |
| **Language** | Hindi, Indian English, Marathi, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Assamese, Gujarati, Nepali, Malayalam, Tamil or US English. It follows the voice you pick. |
| **Speed** | 0.5 to 1.5. Presets: **Slow & clear** (0.8), **Normal** (1) and **Brisk** (1.2). |
| **Sample rate** | 8 kHz (telephony), 16 kHz, 22.05 kHz or 24 kHz. 24 kHz is the default. |

The Raya panel also gives writing advice. Write Hindi in Devanagari script, because Romanised Hindi is not supported. Write Indian names in Devanagari even inside English sentences. It offers a prompt snippet you can copy if another language model writes your agent's lines.

## TTS Switcher

**TTS Switcher** (in the Voice Output group of Advanced Settings) lets the agent change its voice during a call. Click **Add** to create a switch.

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Tool Name** | A unique name, for example `switch_to_hindi`. It becomes the tool name the model sees. |
| **Description** | When the agent should switch, for example "Switch to Hindi voice when user speaks in Hindi or requests Hindi". The model reads this to decide. |
| TTS provider, voice and settings | The voice to switch to. |

To switch the voice and the speech-to-text together, use the language switcher in [Languages](./languages).
