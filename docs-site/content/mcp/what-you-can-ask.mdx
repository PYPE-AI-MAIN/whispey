title: What you can ask through MCP
description: Example requests for your AI assistant once Whispey is connected — call analytics, transcripts, costs, campaigns, knowledge base and agent building.
---

Once [Whispey is connected](./overview), ask your assistant in plain words. It picks the right Whispey tool by itself. Name the agent if you have more than one, and give a time range when it matters.

## Calls and trends

- "How many calls did we get last week, and how many succeeded?"
- "Show me a chart of daily call volume for the last 30 days."
- "What was the average call length this month compared with last month?"
- "Compare the Reminder agent with the Booking agent."

## Individual calls

- "Find yesterday's calls that lasted under 10 seconds."
- "Show me the transcript of the longest call yesterday."
- "Why are calls failing today?"

Transcripts are read one call at a time. For a pattern across many calls, ask for counts or a breakdown first, then read a few examples.

## Outcomes and quality

- "Break down this week's calls by final disposition."
- "Check whether our 'not interested' labels match what callers actually said."

The second kind of request compares your [dispositions](../analytics/dispositions) with the transcripts and points out labels that look wrong.

## Costs

- "What did we spend last month, and on what?"
- "Which agent costs the most per call?"

## Campaigns

- "Give me a summary of the current campaign."
- "Is the campaign automation running smoothly?"

## Knowledge base

- "What does the Clinic agent's knowledge base say about cancellations?"

## Building and changing agents

Needs building access on your key. The Pype team sets this up. See [Build an agent with AI](./build-an-agent).

- "Create an appointment reminder agent for a dental clinic."
- "Build a flow that books appointments and transfers upset callers to a person."
- "Switch my agent's voice to Raju."
- "Add a disposition for the callback time."

## Ready-made prompts

Your assistant may list these in its prompts menu (in Claude, the **+** or **/** menu):

| Prompt | What it does |
|---|---|
| **weekly_report** | A weekly report for one agent: volume, success, cost and trends. |
| **compare_agents** | Compares agents side by side. |
| **investigate_issue** | Looks into a problem you describe, using calls and transcripts. |
| **pype-create-agent** | Starts building a new agent. Builder keys only. |

## Tips

- **Give dates.** "Last 7 days" or "1 to 15 October" gets a precise answer.
- **Ask for the next step.** After a count, ask "show me three examples" to read the calls behind it.
- **Charts.** Ask for "a chart". Assistants that can draw charts will.
- **Results are capped** at a few hundred rows per question. For a full export, use [Exporting data](../analytics/exporting-data).
