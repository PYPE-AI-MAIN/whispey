**Agent Studio** is the home of an agent you built with your AI assistant. It is a place to try the agent and watch it work: start a call, hear it, and see the result. Changes to the prompt and behaviour are made by asking your assistant. See [Build an agent with AI](./build-an-agent).

Open it from the left menu: choose your agent, then **Agent Studio** under **Build**.

![Agent Studio: the test call panel on the left, results on the right](/img/studio/workspace.jpg)

## Who sees it

**Agent Studio** appears for agents built through MCP or with [Ask Pi](../ask-pi/overview). Owners and admins also see it for other agents made with Pype.

If the agent was not built through MCP, Studio shows how to do it instead.

![The Studio page for an agent not built through MCP](/img/studio/empty-state.jpg)

Click **See what Studio looks like with sample data** to look around anyway.

## Call your agent

1. Click **Setup** at the bottom to check the voice and test values. See [Before you call](#before-you-call).
2. Click **Start call**. Allow your browser to use the microphone.
3. Talk. The status shows **Listening**, **Thinking** or **Speaking**, and the conversation appears as you go.
4. Click **End call** when you are done. **Mute microphone** mutes you without ending the call.

Only **Voice** works today. **Phone** and **Chat** are marked **Soon**.

## Before you call

**Setup** has two parts.

![Setup open, with the voice and the test values for each variable](/img/studio/setup.jpg)

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Voice** | Pick a voice. Click play to hear it first. Choosing a voice changes the live agent and restarts it, which takes a few seconds. |
| **Variables** | Test values for the agent's variables, such as the customer's name. Empty ones say **Not set**. |

When you change variables, the button reads **Update and start**. Clicking it saves the values to the agent, then starts the call.

:::note
Choosing a voice or pressing **Update and start** changes the real agent, not just your test. Real calls use the new voice and values from then on.
:::

While the agent restarts, the button shows the progress. If someone is changing the agent elsewhere, for example through your AI assistant, Studio waits: **An update is currently in progress — try again in a moment.**

## After the call

**Last call** shows the conversation. When the page opens, it shows the agent's most recent call, which may be a real one. Click the arrow icon (**View full details**) to open the full trace.

Right after you hang up, it says **Saving call…** while the call is stored. If it takes longer than about a minute and a half, it says the call will appear in [Call Logs](../analytics/call-logs) shortly. Click **Check again**.

## What the agent pulled out

The **Dispositions** panel on the right shows the values from the last call, such as the outcome or a callback time.

- Values appear as soon as you hang up. They are worked out from the conversation on the spot, then replaced by the saved values once the call is processed.
- While a call is on, it says **Call in progress — values from this call will show up once it ends.**
- Click **Dispositions** to change which details are pulled out. See [Dispositions](../analytics/dispositions).

**Overall** shows how many calls the agent has had, including test calls.

## Rename the agent

Click the pencil next to the agent's name at the top. Press Enter to save or Esc to cancel. Your AI assistant cannot rename agents, so do it here.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| We couldn't access your microphone… | Allow microphone access for this site in your browser, then try again. |
| The agent didn't join the call… | It may still be starting. Wait a few seconds and try again. |
| Connection dropped — reconnecting… | Wait. It reconnects by itself. |
| We couldn't apply that voice… | Try again. If it keeps failing, pick another voice. |
