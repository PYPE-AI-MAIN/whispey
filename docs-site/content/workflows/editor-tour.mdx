## The top bar

The bar at the top of the editor, with each control numbered:

The problems button is red and says "to fix" when Deploy is blocked until you fix something. It is grey and says "to check" when it is only advice. Click it for a list.

### The Setup menu

| Item | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Voice, model and language** | How the agent sounds, which AI model it uses, how it behaves, and whether it answers from your documents. |
| **Variables** | The details the agent knows or collects. |
| **Rules for any step** | [Global rules](./global-rules). |
| **Knowledge base (documents)** | Upload documents and switch them on. |
| **Webhook (send calls to your system)** | Send each finished call to your own address. |
| **Version history** | Every deploy, with a button to restore one. |

## The canvas

- **Step boxes** show the step name and type. A green START label marks the first step.
- **A plus button** appears to the right of a step when you hover it. Use it to add the next step. See [Connections](./connections).
- **Fit view** zooms out to show the whole flow.
- **Go to start** centres the canvas on the first step.
- **Auto-arrange** lays the steps out from left to right in call order.
- Drag the canvas to move around, and scroll to zoom.

Every connection is always drawn. In a long flow, click a step and the others fade, so you can follow that step's own connections.

## The side panel

Click the panel icon to open it.

- **Add** lists step types you can drag onto the canvas. The plus button on a step is usually quicker.
- **Outline** lists your steps in call order, grouped by stage, with START marked. Click one to jump to it.

## The step panel

Click a step to open its settings on the right. At the top it explains what the step type does. If the step has a problem, such as nothing leading to it, a yellow box says so and offers a way to fix it.

## The checklist

The **Get your agent live** card walks you through describing, fixing, voice, documents, deploy and test. You can collapse it or close it with the cross.
