---
title: Ask Pi in Call Logs
description: Get counts, summaries and filters for an agent's calls by asking in plain words, and know what Pi reads to answer.
---

On an agent's [Call Logs](../analytics/call-logs), the **Ask Pi** button at the bottom right opens a chat about that agent's calls. Ask it what you would otherwise find by filtering and reading calls one by one.

![Ask Pi open on the Call Logs page](/img/call-logs/ask-pi.jpg)

Everyone who can edit the agent sees the button. Viewers do not.

## Good questions to ask

| You want | Ask |
|---|---|
| A count | "How many voicemail calls today?" |
| A breakdown | "What were the outcomes of this week's calls?" |
| What happened | "Summarise today's connected calls." |
| Why something failed | "Why did yesterday's failed calls fail?" |
| A filtered table | "Show me flagged calls." |
| About one call | Open the call first, then "Why did this call end?" |

Pi knows the dates you picked in **Period** and the filters you applied, and works within them unless you name other dates in your question.

## How Pi answers

Pi picks the cheapest way to answer, so counts are exact and summaries stay fast.

**Counts and breakdowns** come from the call data itself: status, duration, flags, tags and your [dispositions](../analytics/dispositions). Pi counts every matching call, up to 20,000. It does not read transcripts for this, so "How many calls were interested?" is exact if you have an interest disposition.

**Summaries and "why" questions** need the conversations. Pi reads a sample of up to 50 matching calls, and says how many it read out of how many matched. For a long call it reads the first part of the transcript.

:::tip
Dispositions make Pi much better. If you keep asking the same question about calls, such as "did they ask about price", add it as a [disposition](../analytics/dispositions). Then Pi can count it across every call instead of reading a sample.
:::

**About the open call:** when a call is open, Pi already knows which call you mean. Ask "this call" without pasting anything.

## Filters from Pi

When the answer is a set of calls, Pi offers a filter instead of changing your table.

1. Ask, for example, "Show me calls longer than 2 minutes that were not interested."
2. Pi shows a card with the filters it would add.
3. Click **Apply** to use them, or **Dismiss** to ignore them.

![A filter suggested by Pi, waiting for Apply](/img/call-logs/ask-pi-filter.jpg)

Applied filters work like your own. They show under **Filtered by**, where you can change or remove them. Your **Period** stays as it was.

A count question, such as "How many…", gets a number, not a filter.

## What Pi does not do here

- It does not change calls, tags or flags.
- It does not apply anything without your click.
