**QA Audit** is where problems with calls get fixed. You flag a call that went wrong. The QA team reviews it, may hand it to a prompt engineer, and tells you what was done. You can also ask the QA team to review a whole week of calls.

Open it from the left menu: choose your agent, then **QA Audit** under **Monitor**. Anyone who can see the agent can open the page.

It has two tabs: **Flagged calls** and **Weekly review**.

## Flag a call

You can flag a call from three places:

- The **Flag** button on a row in [Call Logs](./call-logs).
- The **Flag** control in the top bar of an open call.
- The **Flag** control on the [call trace page](./traces-and-timeline).

1. Click **Flag**. A box titled **Report an issue** opens.
2. Describe what went wrong. Be specific. For example: "Response at 2:30 was incorrect". A time in the recording helps the reviewer.
3. Click **Add flag**, or press Cmd and Enter.

You see **Flagged. Our QA team will review it.** with a link to check the status. The flag becomes a ticket on the **Flagged calls** tab with the status **Pending**, and the QA team is notified.

You can add more than one flag to a call.

### Change or remove a flag

- You can edit the text of your own flag. Open the flag and use the pencil icon.
- You can remove your own flag. People who can edit can remove anyone's flag.
- Editing or removing a flag changes its ticket only while the ticket is still **Pending**. Once the QA team has started on it, the ticket stays as it is.

## Flagged calls tab

The tab lists every flag on this agent, newest first, 25 to a page. Above the list are filters with a count for each:

| Filter | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every ticket. |
| **Pending** | Not started. |
| **In review** | The QA team is working on it. |
| **Resolved** | Finished. |

If nothing has been flagged yet, the tab says so and links you to **Call Logs**.

The table has these columns:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Flag** | What was reported. |
| **Flagged by** | The email of the person who flagged it. |
| **Date** | When it was flagged. |
| **Status** | **Pending**, **In review** or **Resolved**. |
| **Assigned to** | The prompt engineer working on it. Pype staff only. |

Click a row to open it. Inside you see:

- **Open call**, which opens the call's trace page.
- The resolution note, once resolved.
- The history in order: **Flagged**, every **Status** change and every comment from the QA team. Comments can include images.

### What each status means

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | The flag is waiting. Nothing has started. |
| **In review** | The ticket has been passed on, usually to a prompt engineer, to look at and fix. |
| **Resolved** | The QA team has written down what was done. |

You are told when a status changes, by a notification in the app and an email. Look for the bell icon.

## Weekly review

The **Weekly review** tab asks the QA team to review every call from one finished week. They share the result as a Google Sheet.

1. Open **Weekly review**.
2. Read the week named under the heading. It is always the last full week, Monday to Sunday, in India time.
3. Click **Request last week's review**.

The row appears with the status **Requested**. It moves through these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Requested** | Waiting for the QA team. |
| **In progress** | The QA team is reviewing the calls. |
| **Ready** | Done. An **Open sheet** link appears. |

A week can only be requested once. After you ask, the button reads **Requested for last week**. You can only request a week that has finished.

The QA team can add a note for you. It appears under the row.

## Who sees what

| Person | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Customers (project members) | See tickets and weekly reviews for agents they can already see. Flag calls. Request weekly reviews. Read comments and the resolution note. They cannot change a ticket's status. |
| Pype staff | See the same, plus the **Assigned to** column and the internal sheet on a ticket. |
| QA team | Everything above, for every project. They also reply, change status, hand tickets over and update weekly reviews. |

The internal sheet and the assignee are never shown to customers.

## For the QA team

Members of the QA team see an extra box at the bottom of every open ticket:

1. Write a comment. A comment is shown to the customer. For a resolution, the box asks **What was done? (required, the customer sees this)**.
2. Optionally click **Attach image** to add up to 5 images (PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF, 5 MB each).
3. Choose a **Status**: **Pending**, **In review** or **Resolved**.
4. When the status is **In review**, choose a **Prompt engineer** and, if you have one, paste an **Internal sheet (Pype staff only)** link.
5. Click **Send**. The button changes to **Send and move to** the new status when the status changes.

What the status choices do:

- **Pending**: not started. The customer sees nothing new until you comment or change the status.
- **In review**: passes the ticket to the prompt engineer. They are emailed and tagged on Slack.
- **Resolved**: the customer sees your note and gets an email. A note is required.

For weekly reviews, click **Update** on a row to set the status, paste the **Google Sheet link (required for Ready)** and add an optional **Note for the customer**.

Who is on the QA team, and who can be assigned as a prompt engineer, is managed by Pype admins.
