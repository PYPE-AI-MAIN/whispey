This page is about getting things done in [Call Logs](./call-logs). Each section is one job, with the clicks to do it. For every column, filter and setting, see the [Call Logs reference](./call-logs).

:::note
These steps use the new view. If you see a wide table and clicking a row opens a new page, click **Try the new Call Logs** in the toolbar. Your choice is saved.
:::

## Find your way around

The page has two modes.

**The table** is where you look for calls: dates and filters at the top, one row per call.

![The Call Logs table with the toolbar, filters and columns](/img/call-logs/table.jpg)

**The split view** opens when you click a call. The table folds into a list on the left, so you can move from call to call without losing your place.

![The split view: call list, transcript and call data](/img/call-logs/split-view.jpg)

| Area | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Left: the call list | The calls on this page. Click one to open it. **Table** (or Esc) goes back. |
| Middle: the call | The transcript, the recording player at the bottom, tags and flag at the top. |
| Right: the call data | Dispositions, latency, variables, metrics, metadata and tool calls. |

## See what happened today

1. Click **1D** next to **Period**.
2. Look at **Call Status** and **Duration**. Short calls and any status other than `completed` are the ones to read first.
3. For a count or a summary instead of reading calls one by one, click **Ask Pi** and ask, for example, "How many calls connected today and what happened in them?" See [Ask Pi in Call Logs](../ask-pi/in-call-logs).

## Find calls that went wrong

1. Click **Filter**.
2. Pick **Status**, then **Not equals**, then type `completed`.
3. Click **Apply**.

Other useful filters:

| To find | Filter |
|---|---|
| Calls someone reported | **Flag** · **Has any flag** |
| Very short calls | **Duration (s)** · **Less than** · `10` |
| Slow calls | **Avg Latency (ms)** · **Greater than** · `2000` |
| Calls with a certain outcome | **Transcription** · pick the disposition, for example `final_disposition` · **Equals** · `Interested` |
| One call per customer | **+ Unique by…** · **Customer Number**. Keeps the latest call for each number. |

Filters stay on when you change the dates. Click a chip under **Filtered by** to change it, or **Clear all** to start over.

:::tip
Use the star next to a filter row to pin that field under **Quick add**. Next time it is one click.
:::

## Review a call quickly

1. Click the call. The transcript, recording and call data open.
2. Press Space to play. The line being spoken is highlighted and the transcript follows along.
3. Click any line to jump the recording to that moment.
4. Press J for the next call and K for the previous one. You do not need to go back to the table.

If the call was in another language, switch the transcript between **Original** and **English** at the top.

## Check why a call felt slow

1. Open the call.
2. On the right, open **Latency**. It shows the 75th percentile for the whole call and for each part: speech-to-text, the model, text-to-speech and end of utterance. Red is poor.
3. For the turn-by-turn detail, click **Open the full trace in a new tab** (the arrow icon at the top of the call). See [Traces and timeline](./traces-and-timeline).

## Check what the agent decided

1. Open the call.
2. On the right, open **Dispositions**. These are the answers pulled out of the call, such as the outcome or a callback time. See [Dispositions](./dispositions).
3. Open **Tools** to see each tool the agent called, what it sent and what came back. A failed call has a red mark.

Long values are cut to a few lines. Click **Show more** to read all of it.

## Report a bad call

1. Open the call.
2. Click **Flag** at the top.
3. Write what went wrong and where, for example "At 1:20 the agent quoted the wrong price".
4. Click **Add flag**.

The QA team picks it up from there. Follow it in [QA Audit](./qa-audit).

## Label calls for later

1. Click **+ Add tag** on the call, or in the **Tags** column.
2. Type a name, such as `follow-up`, and press Enter.

Find them again with **Filter** · **Tags** · **Contains tag**.

## Share a call with a teammate

Open the call and copy the address from your browser. It includes the call, so the link opens straight to it. Your teammate needs access to the agent.

To pass on just the id, click the copy icon next to the call id. **Copy transcript** copies the whole conversation as text.

## Keep your own view

- **Columns** picks which columns you see and their order. It is saved for you, for each agent. Use **Sections** to turn a whole group, such as **Metadata**, on or off.
- The row-density buttons switch between **Comfortable rows** and **Compact rows**.
- The view you chose (new or classic) is saved to your account.

## Get calls out

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Send a few calls | Tick them, then **Export CSV** or **Copy JSON** in the bar that appears. |
| Download many calls | **Export** at the top. See [Exporting data](./exporting-data). |

## Shortcuts worth learning

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| J / K | Next or previous call |
| Space | Play or pause |
| Left / right arrow | Back or forward 5 seconds |
| Esc | Back to the table |
| Cmd + ] | Show or hide the call data |

All shortcuts are under the keyboard icon at the bottom right of the table.
