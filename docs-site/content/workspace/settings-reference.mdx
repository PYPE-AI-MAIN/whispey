Open **Settings** in the sidebar. The page is titled **Organization Settings** and shows your organization name under the title.

Viewers do not see **Settings** by default. See [Team and roles](./team-and-roles).

## Team Members

The card is called **Team Members**, with the line "Manage who has access to this organization".

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Member count badge | Shows how many people are listed, for example "4 members" |
| **Invite** | Opens **Invite Team Member**. Owners and admins only. |
| **Current Members** | The table of people. Shows **Member**, **Role** and **Status**. |
| **Search by email** | Appears when there are more than five members |
| Role drop-down | Change someone between **Viewer** and **Admin**. Owners and admins only. |
| Remove, **Reactivate** and **Permanently Delete** | Manage access. See [Team and roles](./team-and-roles). |

The empty state says **No members yet**.

## Danger Zone

Shown to the owner only.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Delete Organization** | Permanently deletes the organization after you type its name. |

You cannot delete your only organization. See [Organizations](./organizations).
