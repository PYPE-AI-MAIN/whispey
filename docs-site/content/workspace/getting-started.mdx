Whispey groups everything you build into an **organization**. Your agents, call logs, phone numbers, campaigns and team all live inside one. This page shows you how to sign in, create your first organization and use the sidebar.

## Sign in

1. Open the Whispey sign-in page.
2. Sign in or create a login with the form on the page.
3. After you sign in, Whispey sends you to an organization.

Where you land depends on your account:

| Your account | Where you go |
|---|---|
| You have no organization yet | The onboarding page, to create one |
| You have one or more organizations | The last organization you opened, or the first one in your list |

The page you land on is the **Agents** page of that organization.

## Create your first organization

The onboarding page is titled **Welcome to Whispey!**. Fill in the card called **Create Your Organization**.

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Organization Name | Yes | Up to 100 characters. Use the name of your team or project. |
| Description (Optional) | No | Up to 500 characters. |
| Environment | No | **Production** or **Development**. |

Choose **Create Organization**. Whispey creates the organization, makes you its **owner**, and opens its Agents page.

Whispey also creates an API key for the organization at the same time. You can see it later on the [API keys](./api-keys) page.

## Join an organization someone invited you to

If a teammate invites you, you get an email with a link. Open it.

- If you are not signed in, Whispey asks you to sign in first, then opens the organization.
- If you are signed in, Whispey opens the organization straight away.
- If the link is old or wrong, you see **Invalid Invite Link**. Ask your admin to send a new invite.

See [Team and roles](./team-and-roles) for how invites work.

## The sidebar

The sidebar is on the left. These are the items you may see.

| Sidebar item | Group | What it opens |
|---|---|---|
| Overview | Top | Organization-wide analytics |
| Agents | Top | The list of your agents. Create and open agents here. |
| Ask Pi | Top | Pi, the built-in assistant. Shows a **Beta** badge and is turned off for people who are not owners or admins. |
| Campaigns | Calling | Batch outbound calls. See [Campaigns](./campaigns). |
| Phone Settings | Calling | Phone numbers assigned to your agents. See [Phone numbers](./phone-numbers). |
| API key | Settings | Your organization API keys. See [API keys](./api-keys). |
| Settings | Settings | Team members and organization settings. See [Settings reference](./settings-reference). |

:::note
You may not see every item. **API key** shows only for owners and admins. **Campaigns**, **Phone Settings** and **Settings** are hidden from viewers by default. See [Team and roles](./team-and-roles).
:::

At the top of the sidebar are links to **Docs** and **Help Center**, and a light and dark theme switch. At the bottom is your user menu, where you can sign out.

The sidebar also has the organization switcher. It shows the name of your current organization. See [Organizations](./organizations).

To make the sidebar smaller, use the collapse button or press Cmd+B. On a phone the sidebar opens as a drawer.
