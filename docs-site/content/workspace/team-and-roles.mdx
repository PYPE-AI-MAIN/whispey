You manage your team on the **Settings** page. Owners and admins can invite people, change roles and remove members.

## Roles

An organization has three roles.

| Role | How someone gets it |
|---|---|
| Owner | The person who created the organization |
| Admin | Chosen when you invite someone, or set later |
| Viewer | Chosen when you invite someone, or set later |

Older accounts may show the roles **member** or **user**. Whispey treats them as viewers.

You can only pick **Admin** or **Viewer** when you invite or change a role. There is one owner, and the owner's role cannot be changed.

## What each role can open

| Area | Owner and admin | Viewer |
|---|---|---|
| Overview and Agents | Yes | Yes |
| Ask Pi | Yes | No. Turned off, with a **Beta** badge. |
| API key page | Yes | No |
| Campaigns | Yes | No |
| Phone Settings | Yes | No |
| Settings page | Yes | No |
| Invite, remove and change roles | Yes | No |
| Delete the organization | Owner only | No |

Inside an agent, viewers also do not see these by default:

- The agent configuration page
- Knowledge base
- Phone calls (call configuration)
- Field extractor, metrics and the re-analyze option in call logs
- The total cost card on the agent overview

Owners and admins see all of them.

:::note
The dashboard has no screen to give one viewer extra access. If a viewer needs a section they cannot see, make them an admin or ask Pype support.
:::

## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings**.
2. In **Team Members**, choose **Invite**.
3. In **Invite Team Member**, enter their **Email Address**, for example `colleague@company.com`.
4. Pick a **Role**: **Viewer** or **Admin**. Viewer is the default.
5. Choose **Add User**.

What happens next depends on the person:

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| They already have a Whispey login | They are added to the organization straight away. |
| They have no login yet | They appear in the list as **Pending**. They join when they sign up with that email. |
| They were removed before | They are reactivated. |

Whispey sends an invite email. If the email cannot be sent, you see a message that says so, but the person is still added.

You cannot invite someone who is already a member.

## Read the members table

The **Current Members** table has these columns.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Member | The email. Your own row says **(You)**. |
| Role | The role. Owners and admins can change it with the drop-down. |
| Status | **Joined** with a date, **Pending**, or **Inactive**. |
| Last column | Remove and reactivate buttons. |

When there are more than five members, a **Search by email** box appears.

You cannot change or remove the owner, and you cannot change or remove yourself.

## Change a role

1. In the **Role** column, open the drop-down on the person's row.
2. Pick **Viewer** or **Admin**.

You see **Member role updated successfully**.

## Remove a member

There are two kinds of removal.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Remove (the bin button on an active member) | Revokes access. The person stays in the list as **Inactive** and you can reactivate them later. |
| **Permanently Delete** (the bin button on an inactive member) | Deletes the member and their history. You cannot undo it. |

To bring someone back, choose **Reactivate** on their inactive row.
